Insignia · Board Presentation · Mariam & Zviad
Who We Designed For

Wealthy. 50+.
Grew up in the physical world.

Before the rush of screen-to-screen life. They don't want another fintech app. They want something that respects their time, their taste, and their world — something that feels real.

What They ValueFor HNW customers, brand feel and trust signals consistently outrank feature count. They are not choosing on parity — they are choosing on dignity.
Material & Craft

We explored materials
to make it feel real.

Leather. Fabric. Paper. Gold. Brushed metal. We kept only the ones that could carry both luxury and simplicity of use. Real gold reflects its environment — so we used the phone's gyroscope to bring it alive. Leather wraps the accounts — because each account, in the customer's mind, is a wallet.

The Thesis
We didn't design an app. We designed an accessory.

A thing that lives in the pocket the way a fine watch or a leather wallet does. So when our customer thinks of their bank — they don't picture numbers and spreadsheets. They picture a thing.

Thank you.

Pick it up. Tilt it. Feel it. Make it yours.
